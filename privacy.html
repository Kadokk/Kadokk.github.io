Privacy policy

Thousand Sunny Reports is a private tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google accounts.

  • It accesses Google data only for accounts the owner has connected, and only to create, store and read the owner's own report files.
  • It does not collect data from anyone else, does not sell or share any data, does not show ads, and does not use Google data to train AI models.
  • Credentials are stored privately on the owner's own server and are never published.
  • The owner can remove access at any time from their Google Account security settings (Third-party connections).

This website stores no data, uses no cookies, and runs no scripts or trackers.
